# Visual System Guide

Use this guide when selecting art direction, motion, typography, transitions, and music.

## Brand Extraction

Extract these from the website before designing:

- Primary and secondary colors
- Background treatment
- Border radius and card density
- Typography style: geometric, editorial, utilitarian, playful, technical
- Icon style
- Product UI density
- Existing motion cues, if any

Use the brand system as the base. Add polish, not a disconnected theme.

## Typography

Use at most three text roles:

- Product or UI text: the product's own font or a close system match.
- Display title: one strong face for major headlines.
- Utility labels and metrics: a clean sans or numeric face.

Variation should create hierarchy, not novelty. A good pattern for AI/SaaS videos:

- Clean sans for labels and body.
- A confident display serif or slightly editorial face for one or two major moments.
- Monospace or tabular numeric styling only for metrics, code, logs, or data.

Check that every text block remains readable at final video scale and platform compression.

## Motion Language

Choose transitions based on meaning:

- Liquid or ripple reveal: transformation, rewriting, generation, creative flow.
- Scan wipe: detection, analysis, review, security, verification.
- Spatial push: moving through product navigation or workflow stages.
- Spotlight reveal: proof, score, key metric, final output.
- Split comparison: before/after, manual/automated, old/new.
- Rail or carousel movement: suite breadth, multiple tools, feature families.

Do not use complex transitions as decoration. The transition should explain why the next scene follows.

## Layout

- Keep product screenshots large enough to inspect.
- Use callouts near the relevant UI, but never on top of important text.
- Match before/after panels by size and scale.
- Leave breathing room around browser frames; avoid stacking cards inside cards.
- Use a consistent safe margin for platform crops.

## Music and Audio

Use instrumental, rights-safe music. Good search terms:

- "royalty free ambient tech instrumental"
- "royalty free minimal future bass instrumental"
- "royalty free corporate technology background"
- "CC0 electronic instrumental"
- "YouTube Audio Library ambient technology"

Document the source, license, track title, author, and URL when music is included.

Audio mix targets:

- Music should support pace without feeling rushed.
- Lower music during voiceover or dense subtitles.
- Add soft UI clicks, whooshes, or scan sounds only when they align with visible action.
- Fade in and out cleanly; avoid abrupt starts.

## Design QA

Check several still frames:

- First frame, first hook, primary action, result, proof, feature suite, CTA.
- No overlapping captions, masks, screenshots, or UI.
- No blurry screenshots at the moment the viewer needs to read.
- No brand color overuse that turns the video into one flat hue.
- No accidental capture tool UI, notifications, or browser chrome artifacts.

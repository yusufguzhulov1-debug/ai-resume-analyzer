# Quality control

## Structural checks

- Final file contains H.264 video and AAC audio.
- Resolution and aspect ratio match delivery.
- Video and audio durations differ by no more than 0.10 seconds.
- Full-file decode exits cleanly.
- Frame rate is constant at 30 fps unless the user requested another rate.

## Visual checks

- Opening question is readable within the first three seconds.
- Every conceptual scene has a content-matched character or an explicit reason to use a pure diagram.
- Character face, costume, palette, and keyline remain consistent across poses.
- Background plates contain no baked-in people that should move independently.
- Hero, support, rear, and foreground layers have clear scale and z-order hierarchy.
- Facing direction, eye lines, feet, hands, props, and ground lines are believable.
- Entrances are staggered and depth layers do not move with identical amplitude.
- Foreground occlusion creates depth without hiding faces, hands, props, or captions.
- Screen crops show only the relevant app, file, or editor; no desktop wallpaper.
- Hybrid screen text is legible on a phone preview.
- Circular PiP does not cover UI, support card, or subtitles.
- Negative space is either intentional or carries a useful command/path/step card.
- Paper texture, red scraps, shadows, and ink landscape remain consistent.
- Diagrams reveal in the order of the narration.
- Subtitles stay within two lines and no particle appears alone.
- Outro holds its final CTA for at least one second.

## Audio checks

- No clipped consonants at speech edits.
- No duplicate phrases left after correction takes.
- No distracting gaps unless intentionally used for emphasis.
- Last spoken word decays naturally before the outro begins.
- 1.1× versions preserve pitch and intelligibility.

## Contact sheet

Extract at least:

1. Opening presenter frame.
2. First full paper concept frame.
3. Representative hybrid screen/PiP/support-card frame.
4. Progressive flow midpoint.
5. Final spoken sentence.
6. Outro entrance.
7. Final CTA frame.

Inspect the sheet at thumbnail size and full resolution.

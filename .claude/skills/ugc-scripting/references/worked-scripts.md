# Worked scripts

The same imaginary product through all six frameworks, at 30 seconds and roughly 72 words each. The product: a tool that turns a written description into a finished video.

Read these for shape, not for lines, when you are choosing a framework or a script feels shapeless. Every specific here ("eleven minutes", "forty launch videos") is invented and must be replaced with the user's own or bracketed. Times are seconds; multiply by 30 for frames.

---

## PAS (problem, agitate, solve)

The default. 7 / 50 / 15.

> **Hook (0.0 to 2.8)** Three hours. For fifteen seconds of video.
>
> **Agitate (2.8 to 8.0)** And it is always the last thing, the night before, when everyone has gone home.
>
> **Turn (8.0 to 9.5)** So I stopped editing and just described it.
>
> **Demonstrate (9.5 to 22.0)** Typed what I wanted. Watched it build the scenes. Changed the headline twice because I could. That is the whole workflow.
>
> **Relief (22.0 to 26.0)** It was done before I finished my coffee.
>
> **CTA (26.0 to 30.0)** Link is in the bio. It is free to try, which is why I did.

Note the agitation is five seconds and stops. Past that it is complaining.

---

## AIDA (attention, interest, desire, action)

For 45s and up. At 30s the middle two collapse, which is why this version feels tight.

> **Attention (0.0 to 3.0)** Nobody told me you could do this in a browser.
>
> **Interest (3.0 to 12.0)** No timeline. No keyframes. You describe the video and it writes the scenes, then you argue with it until it is right.
>
> **Desire (12.0 to 24.0)** I made four versions of the same ad in the time it used to take to export one. Different hooks, same body. That is the part that actually matters.
>
> **Action (24.0 to 30.0)** Go make one. Takes about ten minutes to find out if I am lying.

---

## BAB (before, after, bridge)

Needs a visible transformation. The two states must be shot the same way or the change does not read.

> **Before (0.0 to 8.0)** This is what my launch videos looked like. Screen recording, a title card, and whatever music was free.
>
> **After (8.0 to 18.0)** This is the same product, same script, yesterday.
>
> **Bridge (18.0 to 26.0)** Nothing changed except where I wrote it. I described the video instead of building it.
>
> **CTA (26.0 to 30.0)** It is the first link in my bio.

---

## FAB (feature, advantage, benefit)

Technical product, technical audience. The risk is stopping at Advantage and never reaching Benefit.

> **Hook (0.0 to 3.0)** Every scene is code. That is the whole trick.
>
> **Feature (3.0 to 10.0)** Each scene is a real file that renders the same picture for the same frame, every time.
>
> **Advantage (10.0 to 19.0)** Which means the agent can read it, edit it, and check its own work, frame by frame, without guessing.
>
> **Benefit (19.0 to 26.0)** So when you say the headline is too small, it fixes the headline. Not the whole scene.
>
> **CTA (26.0 to 30.0)** Repo is in the bio if you want to see the code.

---

## 4P (picture, promise, prove, push)

Only with real proof. Without the Prove beat this is a claim with extra steps.

> **Picture (0.0 to 6.0)** Picture shipping the launch video the same afternoon you finish the feature.
>
> **Promise (6.0 to 13.0)** Not a rough cut. The actual thing, with narration, captions and an export.
>
> **Prove (13.0 to 24.0)** Here is the prompt I gave it. Here is the eleven-minute timer. Here is the file.
>
> **Push (24.0 to 30.0)** Try it on something you already shipped. That is the honest test.

---

## Star, story, solution

Needs a character. Useless for faceless formats.

> **Star (0.0 to 4.0)** I have shipped forty launch videos and I hate making them.
>
> **Story (4.0 to 18.0)** Last month we shipped a feature on a Tuesday and the video went out on the following Monday. By then nobody cared. That happens every single time.
>
> **Solution (18.0 to 26.0)** This one went out ninety minutes after the pull request merged.
>
> **CTA (26.0 to 30.0)** If your videos are always late too, go look.

---

## Where each one breaks

| Framework | The failure you will actually hit |
| --- | --- |
| PAS | Agitation runs long and turns into complaining |
| AIDA | Interest and Desire become the same beat said twice |
| BAB | The after state is not visually different from the before |
| FAB | It stops at Advantage and never says what it means for the viewer |
| 4P | Prove is skipped because there was no proof, leaving a bare claim |
| Star, story, solution | The story has no specific detail, so the star is nobody |

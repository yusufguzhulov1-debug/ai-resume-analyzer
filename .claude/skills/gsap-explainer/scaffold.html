Compound interest, in one screen.

Deposit a small amount on a schedule — say $100 a month. Interest is the arrow that turns money into more money: at 5%, $100 becomes $105 without you doing anything. The magic is compounding: that interest starts earning its own interest, so principal and interest can’t stay separate — it’s money on money on money. Guess the total of $100/month for 40 years and most people say ~$48,000 (just the deposits). At 7% it’s closer to $264,000. The lesson: start early — time does the heavy lifting.

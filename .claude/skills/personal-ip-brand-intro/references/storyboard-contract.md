# Storyboard Contract

## Typical 7-Second Arc

This is a communication arc, not a fixed scene template.

| Beat | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Pattern interrupt or provocative statement | 0.5–1.0 s |
| Identity | Creator, category, or brand recognition | 0.8–1.4 s |
| Proof | Offers, tools, process, or expertise | 1.0–1.8 s |
| Point of view | Memorable belief or tagline | 0.8–1.5 s |
| Lockup | Brand ownership and recall | 0.8–1.2 s |

Add, merge, or reorder beats when the music or brand calls for it.

## Text Safety

- Use exact approved wording.
- Keep essential text inside a 5% safe area unless the design deliberately uses edge typography.
- A mask may hide text while entering or exiting, never during its required reading hold.
- Do not split Traditional Chinese words arbitrarily.
- Check the rendered MP4, not only the source or preview.

## Visual Role

Typography, illustrations, and optional images should explain category, process, proof, or personality. Decorative elements must reinforce the same visual language. Prefer two or three recurring motifs over a random assortment of stickers.

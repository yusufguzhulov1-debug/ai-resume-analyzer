# Launch shapes, beat by beat

Read this after picking the shape, while writing the Beats table. Each shape adapts the 30 s default (900f, 120 BPM grid, reveal at 240) from `SKILL.md`; scale the frames proportionally for 15, 45 and 60 s. Frames at 30 fps. The tables are laid out reveal-led; when `launch-taste` step 7 makes the film an accumulation or outcome film, keep the shape's opening and move its 10 to the device's completion at 60–75% (`SKILL.md`, Accumulation or outcome at 30 s).

Opening pace holds in every shape: the idea's device (or its first unit) is on screen and acting by frame 60 (in problem-first, the device is the problem's own object, so it acts from the hook), the first run of units takes ≤ 75–90f, nothing visible stops for more than 1 s between units, and a duration claim is shown compressed (≤ 75f). The setup rows below are budgets to spend on the device, not room for a claim line and a logo card before it starts.

## Problem-first

The product is absent until the turn; the relief rhymes with the opening shot.

| Frames | Beat | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–60 | Hook | The failure state in the user's own world: the unanswered DM, the phone ringing with nobody there, the spreadsheet at 2 a.m. Desaturated or cluttered |
| 60–210 | Agitate | One or two escalations of the same problem, not new problems. ≤5 s total; the viewer already gets it |
| 210–240 | Breath | Stillness or a music dropout: the problem frozen |
| 240–330 | Turn + reveal | The product enters physically (a flood from the problem's own object, an iris from a button), and the palette cleans up |
| 330–720 | Relief | The same problem solved, step by step, on the product's surface |
| 720–900 | Hero + end card | The opening shot again, now calm; mark |

- House: Gojiberry (VO-led, 15 of 20 cuts exit-then-cut, a 6f blue flood from the Send button at the turn); ElevenLabs (paper and dither, 7 pixel-block wipes, VO +3 to +8f after cuts, bed 0.28 as measured; mix to `sound-design`'s bed row today).
- Feed placements: "product absent" means absent as the answer, not absent from the frame. The product's context (its icon in the corner, the device on the desk, the mark as a small header tag) is in frame by 3–4 s; the turn at 240 reveals what it does.
- Goes wrong: agitation longer than a quarter of the film; a turn with a different subject than the problem.

## Demo-first

The product is working at frame 0; the film is one continuous use of it.

| Frames | Beat | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–60 | Hook | Mid-action: a prompt being typed, a mic listening, a file dropping in |
| 60–240 | Response | The product answers; the claim is a 3–5 word line over it |
| 240–330 | Reveal | The most impressive result, through a match-push (6f ease-in to 3.8× into the cut) or a flood from the button that produced it |
| 330–720 | Cascade | Three more results on the same surface, the camera travelling between them |
| 720–900 | Hero + end card | Pull back to the whole product; the mark persists from the last UI element |

- House: Codex (match-push into the mic, a button flood at ×7 exponential zoom over 11f, a spiral collapse; beat interval ~14f; soundtrack only), Notion (11 hard cuts every 13f through a dock, a 14f flash to white, drift multiplied by `1 − white` so it dies before the cut), Claude MCP (camera push 30–78 to 1.7×, pull back to 1.15, push to 2.4× on Send, iris from the go button over 34f), Prequel (the cursor is the handoff between scenes; layout morphs every 50f).
- Goes wrong: a cursor that teleports; a different layout for every feature.

## Manifesto

Copy and the mark only, cut to the music. Family A.

| Frames | Beat | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–90 | Countdown or provocation | "Ready?" or the sharpest line; one word per card |
| 90–600 | The claims | 3–5 claims, each a run of cards; punch words at bleed size (580–960 px), inverted and held longest (12.5–15f) |
| 600–720 | Breath | A beat of black or a slower card |
| 720–900 | Name + mark | The name, then the mark; the mark can hold short (10–30f) by design, or 75f for a site |

- House: "Don't Blink" (76 cards in 600f, 7.9f per card, holds as multiples of a 90 BPM eighth note = 10f, 2-frame strobe on accent cards, hard cuts only, track at 1.0).
- Goes wrong: cards off the grid (feels random, not musical); the punch word not the biggest thing in the film.

## Metric-first

A true number carries the open; the product earns it.

| Frames | Beat | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–60 | Hook | The number already counting, or its context ("Six months ago this was an idea") |
| 60–330 | Story | Idea → shipped → spread, 3–4 beats of 60–90f, persisting element between them |
| 330–420 | Breath + number | The hero number enters (12f) |
| 420–570 | Count | 120–150f ease-out cubic; land punch 1.06 (5f up, 13f down) on 570 |
| 570–780 | Meaning + proof | What it means, investors or customers 60–90f |
| 780–900 | Mark | Mark, held |

- Peak is the land (≈63%), later than other shapes; the build is the story act. Count-up rules: `announce-milestone`.
- House: Lovable funding (shared elements across all 5 cuts, a gradient band that wipes, becomes an underline, then a divider; stat counters 40–48f staggered 10f).

## Category-creation

A reframe before any product shot.

| Frames | Beat | What is on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–240 | The reframe | A world of things the viewer knows (a ring of photos, apps, objects) that resolves into the new word |
| 240–330 | Name the category | The headline, the reveal |
| 330–720 | What lives in it | 2–3 examples, opening from cards to full bleed (card morph 18–28f) |
| 720–900 | Mark | Shared element into the lockup |

- House: Google generative AI (7 tiles on an ellipse sweep 100° in 52f, velocity blur 0.11 × px per frame capped at 26; a colour-field push 23f that the next scene opens on and carries 46 px of momentum; a card-to-full-bleed morph 22f; the mark lands on a gentle spring 16f, scale 0.72 → 1, and persists into the end card).

## 90 s structure (2700f)

Only when there are two stories (before and after the launch, or two distinct workflows).

| Frames | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0–90 | Hook |
| 90–600 | Setup; proposition by 600 |
| 600–690 | Reveal (≈25%) |
| 690–1350 | Demonstration, part one (2–3 runs) |
| 1350–1650 | Turn or escalation: the second story, with its own breath and micro-peak |
| 1650–2310 | Demonstration, part two |
| 2310–2490 | Proof |
| 2490–2580 | Hero |
| 2580–2700 | End card |

VO ≤200 words at 2.3 w/s. If part two is not a different story, cut to 60 s.

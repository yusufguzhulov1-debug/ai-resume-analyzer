# Venn

**Best for:** overlap between sets, when the intersection is the point.

## Layout

- **Three circles is the ceiling.** Four is unreadable, and nobody has ever
  learned anything from one.
- **Fills at 22% in `SERIES` colours**, strokes at 60%. The overlaps then darken
  naturally, which is the whole mechanism; adding a separate colour for the
  intersection destroys it.
- **The centre is labelled, and it is the only reason to draw a Venn.** A big
  sans name and one line of mono under it. If the centre has no name, the
  diagram has no thesis.
- **Set names sit outside their circles**, pushed out along the axis away from
  the centre, so they never land in an overlap.

## Budget

Three circles, one labelled intersection. Labelling the two-way overlaps as well
is almost always too much for a video.

## The reveal

Fills 10 frames apart, strokes drawing around each circle, then the set names,
then the centre last. The centre arriving last is the shot: three things, and
then the thing they make.

## Anti-patterns

- Area-proportional circles. Nobody can read area, and the layout stops working.
- Four sets.
- A Venn where the overlap is empty, which is two circles and a sentence.

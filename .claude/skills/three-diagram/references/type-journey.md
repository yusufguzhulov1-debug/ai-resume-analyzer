# User journey

**Best for:** what a person does across the stages of an experience, and how it
feels.

## Layout

- **The emotion line is the content.** Stages and actions are context for the
  two places it dips, and those dips are what the work is.
- **Three bands down the frame**: stage cards at the top, the action in the mono
  under each, the emotion line plotted below.
- **A y axis with two ticks only**, `BAD` and `GOOD`. A numbered satisfaction
  axis invites an argument about whether it is 3 or 4 and loses the point.
- **The pain points are the accent**: the stage card takes `focal`, and the dot
  on the emotion line is larger and fully opaque.
- The line itself is a 4px accent series drawing left to right.

## Budget

Six stages, three rows, two pain markers. Two: if everything hurts, nothing is
prioritised.

## The reveal

Axis, stage cards 6 frames apart, actions, then the emotion line drawing across
over 34 frames, then the dots. The line drawing through the dips, after the
viewer already knows the stages, is the shot.

## Anti-patterns

- A smooth curve through the points, which invents feelings between stages.
- Five pain points.
- Channels, touchpoints, opportunities and KPIs as extra rows. That is a
  workshop artefact, not a video.

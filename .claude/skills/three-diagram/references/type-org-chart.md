# Org chart

**Best for:** who owns what, who routes to whom, and where it escalates. Human
teams, or agent teams.

## Layout

- A tree in shape, `flow: "vertical"`, but **the sublabel is the content**. A
  name with no remit is a box, and a reader learns nothing from a box. Write the
  remit: `ships features`, `keeps it up`, `pages, 24/7`.
- **The accent is the routing point**, not the most senior person. The question
  this diagram answers is "where does my thing go", and seniority rarely answers
  it.
- **Draw the dotted-line report.** Every real organisation has one and most
  charts hide it. Dashed, labelled with the trigger (`30 MIN`), not with the
  relationship.
- `security` kind for an on-call or duty role; `external` for an escalation
  target outside the team.

## Budget

Twelve nodes, four levels. Both ceilings are generous and both are usually the
wrong target: six boxes that each mean something beat twelve that do not.

## The reveal

Top down, 7 frames apart, then the reporting lines, then the escalation path
last. The escalation is the one anyone remembers.

## Anti-patterns

- Headshots or avatars. This is a routing diagram.
- Drawing the whole company. Draw the part the video is about.
- Vacancies as empty boxes. Either the role routes work or it does not exist.
